##Pricelists, Promos & Cash In / Cash Out##
ᴃᴃTop-right menu ➔ 'Cash In / Cash Out' & Numpad action buttonsᴃᴃ
While working inside the register, daily store events happen:
- **Pricelists:** If a VIP customer walks in, Odoo applies their customer pricelist, or the cashier can click **Pricelist** to switch rates manually.
- **Promo Codes:** Cashiers can tap **Action ➔ Enter Code** to apply discount coupons or loyalty vouchers.
- **Cash In / Cash Out:** If the manager takes $50 from the drawer to buy store cleaning supplies:
-- We tap the top-right menu ➔ **Cash In / Cash Out**.
-- Select **Cash Out**, enter $50, and type the reason: *'Store cleaning supplies'*.
-- Odoo logs an immediate timestamped audit note so no cash ever goes missing.
