??**Temporary & Holding Accounts**??
ᴃᴃChart of Accounts ➔ Filter by Account Type (Liquidity Transfer & Outstanding Accounts)ᴃᴃ
To keep our bank and company transactions accurate, Odoo uses **Temporary Accounts**.
¿¿A Temporary Account acts as a bridge to hold money in transit. Once the transaction is fully processed and reconciled, its balance always clears back to zero¿¿
- **Liquidity Transfer Account: ᴃᴃSKIPᴃᴃ**
-- Used for internal transfers between two company bank accounts.
-- When Bank A sends money, Bank A is Credited and Liquidity Transfer is Debited.
-- When Bank B receives money, Bank B is Debited and Liquidity Transfer is Credited.
-- Both bank statements reconcile independently, and the transit account zeroes out.
- **Outstanding Receipts & Payments**:
-- These accounts simply hold the money while we wait for transactions to actually clear in our bank.
-- When we record a customer payment, Odoo marks the invoice as In Payment, and the money waits in Outstanding Receipts.
-- Later, when our bank statement comes in and we match it, the funds move into our Real Bank Account, and the invoice officially becomes Paid.
-- For this to work we have to go to our journal and bank
--- For **__Incoming payments__** we need to set **__outstanding receipts__**
--- For **__Outgoing payments__** we need to set this to **__outstanding payments__**
​
- **Bank Suspense Account: ᴃᴃSKIPᴃᴃ**
-- Acts as the temporary parking room for imported bank transactions until our accountant matches them to an invoice or expense account."

**??Asset Models??**
ᴃᴃConfiguration -> Asset Modelsᴃᴃ
Now lets see how **__Asset Models, Asset and depreciation works__**
Usually, our finance teams spend a lot of time tracking equipment in Excel and calculating monthly depreciation by hand. In Odoo, we automate this entire process using **Asset Models**.
​
The Asset Model generally acts like a template:
​
- **__Depreciation Method__**: Here we define the method on how to calculate depreciation. we can choose:
-- **Straight Line**
-- **Declining**
-- **Declining then Straight Line**
Straight line means the depreciation of the asset will be equal on each year. for declining method for some initial years the depreciation is higher as the asset value decreases so the depreciation also decreases. and for Declining then Straight Line method first few years it follows declining method after that it switches to Straight Line for all remaining periods
- **__Duration__**: it shows the number of depreciations to depreciate our asset
- **__Computation__**: 
-- **No Prorata**: Here it means that even if we purchase the asset in the middle of a year it will still consider the full 12 month of that year
-- **Constant Periods**: Here it treats every single month as same in length. For example we have some month like  February with 28 days but here it will consider each month with same length.
-- **Based on days per period**: Here odoo breaks the depreciation down into a daily rate. It considers each days of the month.
- On the right side, under **Accounting**, we link our balance sheet accounts like: 
--- **Fixed Asset Account**
--- **Depreciation Account**,
--- and our monthly **Expense Account**.
--- For journals we generally use **misc operation**
​
Also when we purchase an asset it will not automatically be considered an asset. for that we have to
-- go to our **@@__Fixed Asset Account__@@**
-- go to **__Automation__** TAB
-- We have to choose between **@@__create in draft__@@** or **@@__create and validate__@@**

##1. Asset Values & Grouping##
- **__Original Value__**: IT is the total purchase price of the asset before any depreciation.
- **__Acquisition Date__**: The exact date our company bought and acquired the asset.
- **__Asset Model__**: The template we selected earlier, which automatically fills in our depreciation rules and accounts.
- **__Asset Group__**: This allows us to categorize assets —> for example, grouping all computers under **IT Hardware** or heavy equipment under **Factory Machinery** for cleaner financial reporting.

##2. Current Values & Salvage Value##
Looking at the top right section under **Current Values**:
- **__Not Depreciable Value__**: This is the residual Value that will **never** be depreciated.
- **__Book Value__**: This shows sum of depreciable value, Salvage value and book value of all value increase items.
​
##3. Prorata Date & Value at Import (Migration)##
- **__Prorata Date__**: This is the starting date from which depreciation begins counting.
​
- **__Value at Import (Depreciated Amount)__**: 
¿¿This is extremely useful when migrating from our old accounting software into Odoo.¿¿
If we buy an asset that was already partially depreciated we can insert the amount here.
