# System Specs & Gaming Capability (2000-2026)

## Hardware Specifications
- **CPU:** AMD Ryzen 5 3400G (4 Cores / 8 Threads)
- **GPU:** AMD Radeon RX Vega 11 Graphics (Integrated)

## Gaming Capability (2000-2026)
Your system is an entry-level APU setup. Here is what you can expect:

### 2000 - 2012 (Golden Era)
- **Performance:** Excellent.
- **Examples:** *Half-Life 2, Portal, Call of Duty 4: Modern Warfare, Skyrim (Original), Fallout 3/New Vegas, Mass Effect Trilogy.*
- **Settings:** High to Ultra at 1080p.

### 2013 - 2018 (Mid-Range Era)
- **Performance:** Good to Playable.
- **Examples:** *GTA V, The Witcher 3, DOOM (2016), Overwatch, Rocket League.*
- **Settings:** Medium to High at 720p/1080p (depending on the title).

### 2019 - 2022 (Modern Era)
- **Performance:** Challenging.
- **Examples:** *Valorant, CS:GO/CS2, League of Legends, Minecraft, Genshin Impact.*
- **Settings:** Low to Medium at 720p/900p. AAA titles (like Cyberpunk 2077) will struggle significantly even on lowest settings.

### 2023 - 2026 (Current/Future)
- **Performance:** Very Limited.
- **Examples:** Mostly indie games, 2D titles, or highly optimized competitive games.
- **Note:** You will likely need to rely on cloud gaming (GeForce Now, Xbox Cloud) for modern AAA releases.
